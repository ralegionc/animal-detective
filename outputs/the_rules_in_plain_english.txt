What the computer worked out, written as a game of twenty questions
===================================================================
Start at the top. Answer each question about your animal and follow the
branch. Where the arrow appears, that is the computer's answer.

Does it feed its babies milk?
  YES ->
        => say MAMMAL   (60 animals landed here, 100% of them are mammal)
  NO ->
        Does it have feathers?
          YES ->
                => say BIRD   (32 animals landed here, 100% of them are bird)
          NO ->
                Does it have fins?
                  YES ->
                        => say FISH   (26 animals landed here, 100% of them are fish)
                  NO ->
                        Does it have a spine?
                          YES ->
                                Does it live in water?
                                  YES ->
                                        => say AMPHIBIAN   (18 animals landed here, 61% of them are amphibian)
                                  NO ->
                                        => say REPTILE   (13 animals landed here, 100% of them are reptile)
                          NO ->
                                Can it fly?
                                  YES ->
                                        => say INSECT   (18 animals landed here, 100% of them are insect)
                                  NO ->
                                        => say INVERTEBRATE   (26 animals landed here, 85% of them are invertebrate)
